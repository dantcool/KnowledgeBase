One joule is equal to the energy required to accelerate a 1-kg object using a force of one **newton**, the SI unit of force, over a distance of one meter ($1J=1k\;gm^2/s^2$). One calorie is the energy needed to raise the temperature of 1 g of pure water by 1 degree Celsius.
