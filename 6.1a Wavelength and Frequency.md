**Electromagnetic Radiation** - energy that travels through space as waves that are made up of magnetic and electric fields oscillating at right angles to one another. Examples(Visible light, ultraviolet radiation, radio waves)

Waves are characterized by their wavelength, frequency, and speed. 
- Wavelength(symbolized by $\lambda$)  is the distance between two consecutive peaks or through in a wave. 
- Frequency of a wave(symbolized by $\nu$) is the number of complete waves that pass a point in space in a given amount of time. (Hz)
- amplitude - the maximum positive displacement from the medium to the top of the crest of a wave

wavelength and frequency are related by the **speed of light** which is the speed at which all electromagnetic radiation travels in a vacuum. (2.998*10^8 m/s) equal to the wavelength (in meters, m) times the frequency(in Hz 1/s)
$$c=\lambda\nu $$
