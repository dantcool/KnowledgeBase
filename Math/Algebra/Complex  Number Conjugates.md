This is what a regular complex number looks like 
$$z=a+bi$$
where a is the real part and b is the imaginary part but it is the scalar of i.

now for the conjugate it is simply 

$$\overline{z}=a-bi$$
this is useful for simplifying the division of complex numbers 
also when multiplying a complex number with its conjugate the result will be a real number. 

Example 
$$z=7-5i \hspace{1cm} \overline{z}=7+51$$
$$(7-5i)(7+5i)$$
$$(-5i)(5i)=-25i^2$$
because i squared is equal to -1
$$-25*-1=25$$
$$49+35i-35i+25 = 74$$
