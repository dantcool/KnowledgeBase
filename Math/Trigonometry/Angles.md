A ray is a directed line segment, basically a line with the direction. The beginning of the ray would be called the endpoint of the ray and we can place another point on it giving it a symbol and again declare the direction of the line using the symbols .

Now when we have to rays that share an endpoint we get an angle, also the common endpoint of the 2 rays becomes known as the vertex.
