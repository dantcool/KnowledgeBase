**Wave functions $\psi$** - An equation that characterizes the electron as a matter wave 

- The square of a wave function($\psi^2$) is equal to the probability of finding an electron a given region around the nucleus of an atom.

**Orbitals** - The matter wave for an allowed energy state of an electron in an atom or molecule
