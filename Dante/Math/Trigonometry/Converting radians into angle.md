
A full circle rotation is equal to $\downarrow$

$$2\pi \text{ radians} = 360^\circ$$
Radians are basically just the radius of a circle but imagine that we bend them and place them as the arcs of the angle. Thats why 2 of them make a full circle. 

$$\pi \text{ radians} = \frac{360^\circ}{2} = 180^\circ$$
$$ 1 \text{ radian } = \frac{180^\circ}{\pi} \approx 57.3^\circ$$
