Derivatives are functions that give you the slope at any x 

The derivative of any constant is always zero 
$\frac{d}{dx}(c) = 0$


To find the derivative of a monomial such as $x^2$ we use the power rule

$\frac{d}{dx}(x^n)=nx^{n-1}$
our derivatse of x^2 is 2x

$\frac{d}{dx}(\frac{f}{g}) = \frac{gf'-fg'}{g^2}$
