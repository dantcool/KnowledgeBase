Phase change 

When a solid such as ice as heated and melts, the heat energy is used to overcome the forces holding the water molecules together in the solid phase. The ehat energy is therefore change into chemical potential energy. This potential energy can be reconverted to thermal energy when the forces between water molecules form agai, that is, when liquid water freezes to form ice.



The energy to effect a phase change has been measured for a number of substances. 
The **Enthalpy(heat) of fusion** ($\Delta H_{fus}$) for a substance is the energy needed to melt 1g or 1 mol of that substance. 

Then **Enthalpy (heat) of vaporization** ($\Delta H_{vap}$)  is the energy needed to vaporize 1g or 1 mol of that substance.
