**Photoelectric effect** - exhibited when light is shone on a metal and electrons are ejected from the surface of the metal.

**Photons**- packets of light that travels.

a photon of hig eneegr(short wavelength) can lead to the ejection of electrons. Even low-intensity light with high energy(short wavelenght) will lead to a measurable current. This implies that a photon carries an explicit amount of energy called a quantum of energy and that energy itself is quantized. 

Relationship between frequency and the energy of a photon is given by Plancks equation. 

$$E_{photon}=hv$$
where h is Planck's constant 6.626 * 10^-34 J*s.
