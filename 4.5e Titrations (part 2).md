Third type of acid-base titration calculation involves the determination of the molar mass of an acid or base. An acid base titration can be used to calculate the effective molecular mass of the basic chemical found in the ashes produced by the firewood. 

process of finding the molar mass of ashes 
1. Weight the solid ash to know its mass in grams
2. Dissolve it into a fixed volume (like 100ml) to create a stock solution
3. titrate a small sample with an acid of known concentration to find out how much acid it takes to neutralize it 
4. scale up form the small sample to the total solution volume to find the total moles of base 
5. divide the starting grams by those total moles to find the molar mass (g/mol)

### Oxidation-Reduction Titrations 

an **oxidation-reduction titration** uses an oxidation reduction reaction to analyze a solution of unknown concentration. The equivalence point in an oxidation-reduction reaction occurs when the reactants are consumed and often is detected by a color change in one of the reactants.
