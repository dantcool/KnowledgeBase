"Echo chambers and epistemic bubbles" - Article by Nguyen (2020)

Epistemic? Epistemology? What is that? 

Lets start w/ something more familiar 

Ontology - branch of metaphysics/philosophy concerned with identifying the kinds of things that  actually exist it involves categorization and asks questions like 
- what is existence 
- what is nature of existence 
- what do we know? 

Epistemology - another branch of metaphysics/philosophy but it is concerned with the nature of knowledge itself, asks questions like 
- how do we know what we know 
- how do we know truth?

Epistemic Bubble (EB's)
 a limit to information created by our approach to knowledge seeking 
 "a social epistemic structure which has inadequate coverage through a process of exclusion by omission" - (Nguyen, 2020 p 143)

EB results in limited exposure to 
- information 
- difference in perspective 

Nguyen says most of us underestimate the degree to which our exposure to info has been tailored and limited 

Causes of EB
1. Our own tendency to seek like minded info sources (selective exposure)
2. algorithmic, personalized filtering
	1. social media - good for maintaining social relationships/ Not good for unbiased info gathering
	2. Entertainment platforms
	3. Search engines 
	These services give use what the algorithm predicts we want to see based on our previous choices, those in our social group and/or whats trending around us

Implications of EB's
incomplete info cause an increase in deficient understanding causing an increase poor decisions.
incomplete info cause a decrease in quality of understanding which increase quality of decisions

- potential lack of reliable sources(hand picked,like minded friends and family may not provide the best source of info)
- localized search results might not show the whole story. 
- potential manipulation by others (recall the Facebook/ Cambridge analytical scandal from 2015 to 2018, where user data was collected without user consent and used for targeted political ads)
- widespread awareness or underestimation of the effect and level of exposure 
- hard to distinguish the original info source where there is duplicate info // copies of the same thing dont provide corroboration
