An ethical issue of online gambling would b**e exposure it has to all age groups**, especially children. Children are exposed to gambling in video games via loot boxes, in-game cosmetics, and simulated gambling. These loot box mechanics are found in almost all types of video games, and one study has shown that 44-78% of gamers have purchased these loot boxes (Brooks & Clark, 2019,). In a consequential study on whether the purchase of these loot boxes were a “Gateway” to gambling, a survey of 1102 participants was conducted, and of those almost 20% self-reported experiencing gateway symptoms.
