Moral Skepticisim (2 forms)
1. moral nihilism - the view that there are no moral thruths at all. 
2. ethical relativism - view that some moral claims are correct and determine which moral claims are true and which are false

Ethical Relativism comes in two kinds
1. cultural relativism 
2. individual relaivism(ethical subjectivism)

Ethical Objectivism -  the view that some moral standards are objectively correct and that some mora claims are objectively true.
