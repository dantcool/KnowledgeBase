Some helpful Heuristics for using moral inution
----------
1. Be careful with emotions - if heavily emotionally invested in a particular outcome, approach intuition with caution. Check if other, non-intuitive reasons support the same conclusion
2. Kepp it Simple - Intutions about simple things are generally more trustworthy than those  about complex things. Our brrains tend to oversimplify complexity and leave out impotan considerations. 
3. Double-Check Contradictions - If one inutitive judgement contradicts another then examine both carefully when they arrive asyncronously(at different times). Dont automatically prefer the 1st. 

These are heuristics we should only use as guider. They are neither exhaustive nor definative. Sometimes the best use of Moral inuition is not the judgement itself but the fact that we had one. We can let that signal us that something is "off" and we need to dig deeper. Use our other ways of knowing to investigate the situaation and determine the right thing to do.
