Some specific flaws and foibbles in our cognitive approaches
--------------
1. Optimism and overconfidence 
- 90% of drivers think they are above average behind the wheel 

2. Gains + Losses 
- People are loss averse. We hate losing much more than we like winning. It encourages us to be conservative. Sometimes this can be good but sometimes it pressures us NOT to make necessary change

3. Status quo bias 
- We like to keep things the way they are 
	Ex. Subscriptions (Magazines, video streaming services,music streaming)
