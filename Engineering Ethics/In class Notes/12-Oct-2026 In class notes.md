Commons? Shared and finite resources 
Tragedy? Incentivized over use eventually damages or destroys the resource 


Class Example (From hardin, 1968)

Commons X is a grazing pasture for livestock. 
- it can support 10 cows in total (sustainable yield)
- 2 ranchers have access to it and are told they can graze 5 cows each 

What happens if Rancher A decides to place an additional cow on the pasture?
Initially, nothing bad happens 
Rancher A:
- receives all the benefits of an extra cow (if dairy cows, than extra milk)
- receives only a partial penalty/pays only a partial cost and then only eventually due to delay in the system. 
- Cost to A 1/n where n is the number of users of the commons

Rancher B is in the same situation with respect to overuse is the incentive.

When bot A and B use more than their fair share, overuse leads to resource depletion and exhaustion.

"Freedom in a commons brings ruin to all"(Hardin, 1968, p.1244)

Totc is a collective action problem
