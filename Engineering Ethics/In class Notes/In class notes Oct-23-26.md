<u>VIA Classification of character strength and virtues</u>
- represents the work of over 50 top scientist from 2001-2004 
- Captures those human virtues that are valued in all culture and religions across time as reflected in the writings of the worlds major religions and philosophies 
- does not claim to be a comprehensive classification of all possible human virtues. 

Note: VIA originally stood for values in action but now simply part of the name of the associated organization. 
See VIA institute on character www.viacharacter.org
