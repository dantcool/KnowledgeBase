Sources of ethics codes
Where do they come from?
A: Not monolithic $\rightarrow$ codes have different sources 
(This has implications for their strength and enforce-ability)

1)Company/Institutional Codes
Codes of ethics/conduct 
Advantages:
- applies to all employees/associates of that company/institution
- enforce ability is strong 
Disadvantages:
- applicable only to employees/associates of that company/institution 
- potential worry that the code is oriented to the company/institutions self interest 

2)Licensing Boards(For professional License)
In the us, Engineering licensing occurs at the state level 
Advantages:
- good enforcement capabilities 
- specific to each profession (medicine, law, engineering)
Disadvantages:
- there tends to be an operational focus on what can be enforced easily. This may encourage us to consider "Enforceable" things as important. this also narrw the codes to cover lengalistic, beuracratic things
- in the case of engineering only a fraction of the engineering population ears a PE license (about 1:4) 



Sources of Ethic codes 
1) company/institution
2) Licensing boards
3) Profession societies- There a professional societies in every branch of engineering and also CompSci and all have ethic codes
Advantages: 
- applies to all career levels: Students, graduates, EIT, PE, etc
- includes both obligations(must do) and aspirations(should do)
Disadvantages:
- membership is optional 
- enforcement is weak \
