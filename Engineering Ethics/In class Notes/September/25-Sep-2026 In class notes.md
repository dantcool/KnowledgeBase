General points about reinforcing loops
----------------
- R-loops are runaway, amplifying, self-multiplying, reinforcing, exponential 
- 2 kinds of R-loops 
	- Virtuous 
	- Vicious 
- Occur whenever a system has the ability to reproduce. (Often seen only in the interconnections)
- R-loops can lead to exponential growth or decay but few systems can grow or decay indefinitely 

R-loop Examples abound in many systems(Populations, ecosystems, economics, etc.) all clear ties to engineering and coputer science.

General Points about Balancing loops 
-----------
- B-loops are stabilizing 
- They stabilize the stock/variable and thus maintain the current state of the system 
- Sometimes referred to as "Goal seeking"
- This balance can be advantageous (Stability) or problematic (resisiting necessary change)
