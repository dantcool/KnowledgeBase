Causal Loop Diagrams (CLD's)
-----------------------------
why CLD analysis?
- a useful too for visual representation of dynamic interrelationships 
- helps us tst and clarify our thinking 
- helps us to visualize the system with a focus on the interconnections 
- helps us identify recurring patterns (Archetypes)
- Helps us identify where best to make changes (Leverage points)
- helps us understand propagating effects when making changes 
- Gives us a way to simplify model and better grapple with complex problems
- Required for both case analysis assignment

Ingredients of CLD
---------------------------
- variables : words or phrases for things that can change up or down over time 
- Arrows: indicate direction of influence between variables, showing causality 
![[Pasted image 20260923153040.png|245]]
Means A causes B

- polarity/color: blue arrow with pus sign(+) or s(same) means proportional relationship used when A and B change in the same direction(proportionality)

![[Pasted image 20260923153313.png|305]]
Means an increas in A causes and increase in B OR a decrease in A cause a decreas in B
BLue/plus does NOT mean increase it means proportional.


Red arrow with a minus sign(-) or O(opposite) indicates inverse relationships. Used when A and B changes in opoosite directions (inverse)
![[Pasted image 20260923153605.png]]
An increase in A causes a Decrease in B OR a decrease in A cause an increase in B
Red/Minus does NOT mean decrease it means inverse
