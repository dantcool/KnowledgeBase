Important Tips for CLD's
------------------------------------------
- Dont accidentally double-count a causal influene 
![[Pasted image 20260930152729.png|327]]
Unless 1 and 2 describe different causal influences dont include both. If they are seperate cause the an additional (explanatory) variable should be added to justify the 2nd pathway. 

- Check that a feedback loop actually exists before labeling R or B. The arrowheads must point a pathway back to the "First" variable .In above examle  there is no loop.(Note directions of arrow heads)
- Avoid Crossing Lines! Instead, rearrange variable placement or go around with the arrows
- CLDs for ethical analysis are qualitative NOT quantitative.So we use line thickness sparingly and keep things relatively simple 
- some guiding questions to consider while developing a model. (From Meadows,2008,p45-47) - Are the driving factors likely to unfold in this way(as drawn). If they did would the system react in this way (as drawn ) What is driving the driving factors? Identify the causal chains. 

Steps for completing a CLD
-----------------------------
1. Identify the ost significant variables (7<x<_15 or 20 variables is a good rule of thumb for the case analysis assignments)
2. Determine and indicate the causal relationships between variables. What cause what?puc. Take them a pair at a time and consider both directions. Also, dtermine polaritis(proportional or inverse?)
3. ID and label feedback loops (R or B)(empty if the arrow heads dont point a pathway back to the start)
4. Identify and label delays determined by the temporal scale or time horizon of the model. 
5. Look for patterns(Sucg as archetypes, meadows, ch5)
6. identify leverage points (Key places in systems where changes can effctively be made)

This is laid out sequentially (like an algorithim) but in practice it is very iterative.
