Lead-in Discussion on topic of Gambling
-----------
Shows us interesting things about the ways that we think 

Gamble #1: The lottery 
- Get 900 dollars (for sure)
- 90% chance of getting 1100 dollars
Which would you choose 

Typically about 2/3 choose A and 1/3 Choose B

Why A? Small difference not worth the risk. Lost opportunity. 
Why B? More Money. Great odds! Worth the small risk. It didnt cost anything to play 

Gamble #2: The mugging 
- Lose 900 $ for sure 
- 90% chance of losing 1100$ (10%chance of losing nothing)
Which would you choose 

Typically about 1/4 choose to lose 900 for sure and 1/2 choose for 90% chance of losing 1100 dollas and 1/4 abstain from voting. 

Why A? odds are well to lose anyway 
Why B? small chnace of losing nothing is compelling 
Why abstain? Dont like this game (Stay out of the alley)

These 2 examples show something interesting about human thinking 

we are Loss-Averse- we hate losing mouch more than we like winning
