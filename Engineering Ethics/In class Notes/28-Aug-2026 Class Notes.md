Theme 1, unit 1 : Introduction 
---------------------
 Normative Ethical Theories - there are many different ethical theories but in western philosophy there are 3 major categories/types emerge
 
### 1.Consequentialism - (consequence focused)
consoquetialist determine right and wrong based on outcomes (aka consequences) of actions.

flavors
- egoism (maximizing self interest), 
- corporate/institutional Egoism (maximized interest of company/institution), 
- utilitarianism ( greates good for the greatest number of people)

problems
-(w/utilitarianism) likely to neglect or subjugate the nedd and right of individuals and minority groups. 
-(w/consequentialism generally) - it is often hard/impossible to know consequences/risks/benefits in advance, so ethical behavior is a gamble 

### 2.Deontological Ethics(duty-focused)
Deontologist reject consequences and determine right and wrong based on conformity to duties.

flavors
- intuitionism (see stanford Encyclopedia of philosophy SEP)
- Divine Command Theory (see SEP)
- Kantian Ethics (based on the work of immenuel Kant)

Problems 
- focusing to closely on individuals or minority groups makes it hard to see whats best for society 
- when there are conflicts, which duties are paramount?Whose rights take priority?

### 3.virtue ethics (character-focused)
Virtue ethics emphasizes virute or moral charcter,Not rules/duties or consequences 
individual character traits are the foundation of ethical behavior. 
Right actions come from good character traits (virtues)
wrong action come from bad character traites (vices)

EX. Honesty vs dishonosty, Compassion vs Heartlessness 

Flavors
 - sentimentalist accounts(agent-based)
 - pluralistic accounts 
 - aristotelian(class focuse theme)/Neo-Aristotelian  acounts 

Problems
 - with a focus on character it is not always clear what the right action is. 
 - when virtues conflict, which has precedence? 
 - The continum between virtue and vice can be tricky to navigate (eg. honor $\rightarrow$ pride $\rightarrow$ arrogance )
 - no concrete set of virtues ?
