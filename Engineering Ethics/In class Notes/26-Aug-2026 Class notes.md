Intro to Ethics and Ethical Theory 
--------------------
Codes to ethics exist for every branch of engineering and computer science . 
they give us mandates for ethical behavior but do not provide guidance on ambigunece terms nor do they show how the codes should be applied in a given situation. 

An ethical framwork will be needed to use them wisely. 

Bracnhes/Divisions of ethics in philosiphy 
----------------
 Metaethics - objectivism, relativsm - Explores the status, foundations,and scope of moral values. Focuses on the very nature of morality itslef. Unlinke the other 2 branches, which are concerned whit what is or is not moral. 
 
 Moimative ethics - ethical theory  - focuses on theories and criteria for right and wrong actions. 
 
 applied Ethics - Engineering ethics, medical ethics, business ethics - Applies ethical theories to specific problems and/or in practical context


Why Ethical Theroy?
------------
- provides a framework to guide our thoughts and actions
- helps us identify and avoid contradictions
- provides us with a benchmark to measure against
- allows us to examine our pre-reflective beliefs.
