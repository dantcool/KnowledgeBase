### Theme 4, Unit 3: Intellectual Guidance 
Note: The units in this theme overlap and blend together  a bit. 

Readings: 
	Smith + Burgess (2008)
	Schmidt(2014)
	Nguyen(2020), Echo chambers and epistemic bubbles

Requirements for Virtue
- a mean between two extremes(aristotles Golden mean)
- Having the right motive and emotions is essential 
- having the right understanding is also needed $\rightarrow$ Phronesis

Recall that Aristotle described Eudaimonia(Happiness) as rational activity of the soul

"Virtue is not merely useful, agreeable, or admirable trait, but a human excellence and this means it must involve the exercise of reason" - (van tyl, 2019,p.77)

Phronesis/Practical Wisdom(P.W.)
- another Greek word, translated as prudence, ethical judgement, practical wisdom 
- considered an intellectual virtue rather than a moral virtue 
- deeply related to deliboration requiring reflection(System 2 thinking)
- rooted in a natural human capacity "to do the right thing in the right place at the right time and in the right way"-(MacIntyre,cite in Carr, 2004)
- "Allows someone to make intelligent judgments about which actions and emotional responses are appropiate in any particular situation"-(Van Zyl, 2019,p77)

In Aristotelian VE there are 3 "Strong" claims about P.W.

Claim 1: P.W. is neccessary for virtue
	{if we don't at least strive for understanding then we arent really being virtuous}

Claim 2: If P.W is necessary for virtue A, then it must be necessary for Virtue B and Virtue C and all the other virtues 
	{ this one is often contested by other flavor of V.E. since some claim there are virtues we can not understand intellectually, eg Love "The hear has its reason which reason knows nothing of "- Blaise Pascal}.
	However even if this is true, perhaps there is still value in trying to understand such virtues

Claim 3: Sensitivity Requirement: a  virtuous person does not rely on (mere) moral rules r principals, but on discerning morality,or the morally relevant features of practical situations 

Recap of key concpets for Aristotelian Virtue Ethicis
- character is focal 
- virtues are central 
- The Golden Mean(virtues exist on a continuum between 2 extremes)
- Eudaimonia(Flourishing,well-being,Happiness) is key 
- Motivations and emotions must be in harmony with each other and with reason 
- Phronesis(prudence,ethical judgement, practical wisdom) is also essential 

Practical Dimensions of Practical Wisdom 
To achieve P.W. we will need accurate information to make ethical decisions. We must consider how and where we obtain our information and take care to evaluate its merits.
