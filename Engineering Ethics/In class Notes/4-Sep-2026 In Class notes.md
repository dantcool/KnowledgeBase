Theme 2, part 1 : Intuition 
-----------------------
What is intuition?
- judgement that just seems true or correct
- does not originate in perception, introspection, or reason

Moral intuition falls under intuition 

Moral intuition
------------
- more closely resembles thoughts than feelings. 
- can persist over time (we can grow, develop, and train it)

Two mental models of intuition
------------
1. Weak/ diffuse model
	1. intuition(can go to relationship) <-------> facts(can go to background theories)
	2. background theories(can go to facts) <------->relationships(can go to intution)
	3. These are are interconnected
	
	this suggests that our ways of knowing all have biderectional interactions and all are of comparable levels of importance. 
	
2. Strong/ Central Models 
	1. Background theories, facts , Relationships all go to intuition 
	in this case intuition is more foundational and can serve to pass or prevent input from our other ways of knowing. 

Maybe both models are true? 
1st is an ideal case?
2nd is more common, realistic 
 more than 2 models are possible
 prehaps with  practice we can move from the second to the 1st
