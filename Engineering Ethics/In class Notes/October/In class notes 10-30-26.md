EB Epistemic Bubbles 
causes: 
1. Our own habits + biases info choices 
2. use of technology (algorithmic personalized filtering)

Solution: pop the bubble (get outside info) 

How? 
- fact check 
- seek alternative perspectives/ sources 
- expand your social circle to include people who think differently 
- be careful with info from social media 
- learn to use the library's databases more effectively for research 
- use alternative search engines like duckduckgo, brave
- use alternative web browsers like firefox, brave, opera , i use zen 
- use a vpn (carefully and legally)
- use incognito mode/ private tab (no cookies)
- use browser extensions like disconnect, and privacy badger and some ad blockers
- review and adjust your search engine and web browser settings carefully. 
- demand/request/design more transparent algorithimss, user interfaces, provide better defaults 
- educate others/ talk about it

<u>Echo Chambers (EC)</u>
An EC is "An epistemic structure created through the manipulation of trust, it can exist within a healthy information topology(no bubble all info is available) by adding a super structure of discredit and authority" - Nguyen (2020,p.142)

Here its not a case of  missing info but someone is actively discrediting some of it 
"Disparity in trust between members and non members "(p.146)

Examples where EC's might appear 
- cults(extreme case)
- conspiracy theories 
- politics in a 2-party system 
	- followers of  arty ignore/discount/oppose ideas from the other party 

<u>Echo Chamber Characteristics</u> 
- occur intentionally -> intentional(maybe nefarious) control 
- involve disparity in trust between members and non members
- some info is discounted through a process of epistemic discrediting 
- at the same time members epistemic credentials are amplified( the discrediting story anticipates counter arguments and spins them in support )
"Disagreement reinforcing mechanism"
"the existence of contrary beliefs reinforces the original set of beliefs and the discrediting story" (p.147)

EC's are hard to escape because they dont "Erode a members interest in truth, it merely manipulates their credence levels such that radically different sources and instituions will be considered proper sources of evidence" (p.151)

<u>How to Escape ECs?</u>
Need access to someone trusted who is outside the EC to help motivate a "social epistemic reboot"(p.158)
