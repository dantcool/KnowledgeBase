**Thermodynamics** the study of the relationships between heat, energy, and work an the conversion of one into the other. 

When considering chemical events it is useful to define the **system**, the item or reaction of interest, and separate that from the **surroundings**, which include everything else. 

an **isolates system** is one which neither matter nor energy can be passes to or from the surroundings.

A **closed system** is one in which energy but not matter can be passed to or from the surroundings.

**internal energy or $E_{system}$** - energy of the the system 

the total energy of a system cannot be measured directly so the **change in internal energy or $\Delta E_{system}$** is calculated from the following equation. 
$$\Delta E_{system} = q+w\\where\\q=\text{energy in the form of heat exchanged between system and surroundings}\\ \text{w=work done by or on the system}$$
**first law of thermodynamics** - states that the total energy for an isolated system is constant. That is the combined amount of energy and matter in an isolated system is constant. Energy is neither created nor destroyed during chemical or physical changes,but it is instead transformed from one form to another. In other words energy is conserved during a chemical or physical change 
$$\Delta E_{universe}=0 $$
