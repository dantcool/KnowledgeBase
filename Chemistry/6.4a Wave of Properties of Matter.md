<u>The Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle</u>
- Uncertainty principle - states that it is not possible to know with great certainty both an electrons position and its momentum (which is related to its kinetic energy) at the same time 
Mathematical expression of the principle 
$$\Delta x \Delta p \ge \frac{h}{4\pi}\\\Delta x = \text{the uncertainty in the position of the elctron \\}\Delta p = \text{the uncertainty in the elctrons momentum}$$
