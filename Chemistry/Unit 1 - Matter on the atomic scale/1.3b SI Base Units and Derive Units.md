**Length** - the longest dimension of a object is measure in meters(m)
A pencil has a lenght of about 0.16m which is euivalent to 16 centimeter(cm)

**Mass** - the measure of quantity of matter in an object measure in kilograms(kg)
1 kg is about 2.2pounds(lbs). In chemistry lav the mass of a smaple is typically measure using units of grams(g) or milligrams(mg)

**Temperature** - relative measure of how hot or cold a substance is. Temperatures are recorded in Farenheit(F) Celsius(C) or Kelvin(K)

**Volume** - the Si unit of volume is the cubic meter (m^3) but more commonly we use the Liter(L). 
(1mL = 1 cm^3)

**Energy** - The SI unit of energy which is the capacity to do work and transfer heat is the joule (J). Another common energy units is the calorie(cal). 
1 Calorie = 4.184 joules(1 cal = 4.184 J)
1 dietary calorie(Cal) = 1000 calories (1 Cal = 1000 cal = 1 kcal )

**Density** - physical property of a substance that relates the mass of a substance to its volume 
$Desnity = \frac{mass}{Volume}$
densities of solids and liquids are reported in units of grams per cubic centimetere(g/cm^3) or grams per milliliter (g/mL). Gas denisty is reported in grams per liter(g/L). 

The volume of most substances changes with a change in temperature which results in density also chaniging with temperature.
