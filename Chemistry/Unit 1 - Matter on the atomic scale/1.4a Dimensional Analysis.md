**Conversion Factor** - When an equality is written as a ratio
Example 1 hour = 60 minutes both tell the same amount of time. 

**Dimnesional Analysis** - a general problem solving approach that uses the dimenions or unts of each value to guide the calculations being done. 

example with one conversion factor 
48 hours in units of minute (1 hour = 60 minutes)
 48 hours x Conversion Factor = 2900 minutes 

example with more than one conversion factor 
Express the volume of soda in a soft drink can (12 oz) in units of milliliters 
(1 gallon = 128 oz) (1 gallon = 3.7854L) (1 L = 1000mL)

Step 1 write the quantity in its current units and identify the desired new units 
12 oz x Conversion factor = ?mL

Step 2 identify equalities that will conver the given units to the desired units and write them as ratios 

Conversion factor 1 = $\frac{1 gal}{128 oz}$
Conversion factor 2 = $\frac{3.7854 L}{1 gal}$
Conversion factor 3= $\frac{1000 mL}{1 L}$

Step 3 multiply the original value by the converion factors and make sure that the original units are cancelled by the same units in the denominator. The desired units should appear in the numerator 

$$12oz*\frac{1 gal}{128oz}*\frac{3.7854L}{1gal}*\frac{1000mL}{1L} $$
The oz in the first will cancel out the oz in the denominator and this will repeat leaving 

$$12/128 *3.7854*1000=350mL$$
Remember signigicant figures only 2 in this case we got 354 but with sig figs rounded to 350
