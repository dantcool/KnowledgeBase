Significant figures - the digits in a measure quantity that are known exactly, plus one digit that is inexact to the extent plus or minus 1 

Example an orange weighed at a grocery stores has three significant figures (249g) and six significant figures when measure in a laboratory (249.201g)

Example with numbers like 0.03080 and 728060 
1. all non zero digits and zeroes between non zero digits are significant 
2. in numbers containing a decimal point, all zeroes at the end of the number are significant and all zeros at the beginning of the number are not significant
3. in numbers with no decimal point all zeroes at the end of the number are not significant 

So in 0.03080 we have a total of 4 significant numbers and in 728060 we have 5 signifcant numbers. we could then write these numbers as 3.25x10^-4 and 1.200x10^3

**Precision** - how close the values in a set of measurements are to one another 

**Accuracy** - how close a measuremen or  a set of measurements is to a real value
