**Displacement Reactions** are reactions where the number of reactants is typically equal to the number of products. Also called exchange reactions, one atom, ion, or molecular fragment displaces another. Displacement reaction can be single or double reactions. 

### Single Displacement reaction 
In a single displacement reaction one molecular fragment is exchanged for another.

$$AB+X\;\rightarrow\;XB+A$$
Example when solid magnesium is placed in a solution of copper (II) chloride the magnesium displaces the copper. In this case the most reactive metal ,magnesium, replaces the less reactive metal , copper. 

$$CuCl_2(ag)+Mg(s)\;\rightarrow\;MgCl_2(aq)+Cu(s)$$
### Double Displacement Reactions
Also called metathesis reaction occurs when two atoms, ions, or molecular fragments exchange. 
$$AB+XY\;\rightarrow\;AY+XB$$
**Precipitation Reactions** is a double displacement reaction that results in the formation of an insoluble compound. 
Example when aqueous solutions of lead nitrate and potassium sulfate are combine, solid lead sulfate is formed.
$$Pb(NO_3)_2(aq)+K_2SO_4(aq)\;\rightarrow\;2KNO_3(aq)+PbSO_4(s)$$

Many **Acid-Base reactions** also called **Neutralization reactions** are double displacement reaction that result in the formation of water.

$$HCl(aq)_NaOH(aq)\;\rightarrow\;H_2O(l)+NaCl(aq)$$
The H+ and Na+ ions exchange to form the products, water and sodium chloride. 

**Gas-forming reactions** is the reaction between an ionic compound and an acid, are double displacement reactions that result in the formation of a gas, often carbon dioxide(Example below figure 4.1.6)

When hydrochloric acid is added to solid nickel (II) carbonate, a double displacement reaction takes place. 

$$NiCO_3(s)+2HCl(aq)\;\rightarrow\;NiCl_2(aq)+H_2CO_3(aq)$$
The carbonic acid, $H_2CO_3$ formed in the reaction decomposes into water and carbon dioxide gas. 
$$H_2CO_3(aq)\;\rightarrow\;H_2O(l)+CO_2(g)$$
Thus, the net gas-forming reaction between nickel (II) carbonate and hydrochloric acid is written as 
$$NiCO_3(s)+2HCl(aq)\;\rightarrow\;NiCl_2(aq)+H_2O(l)+CO_2(g)$$

Notice that the decomposition of carbonic acid makes this both a double displacement reaction and a decomposition reaction
