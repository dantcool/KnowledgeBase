Examples of using the chart 

(volume to volume)
What volume of 0.35 M NaOH solution is required to neutralize 23.5 mL of 0.5 M HCl solution?
HCl(aq)+NaOH(aq) --> NaCl(aq)+H2O(l)

You are looking for the volume of NaOH solution
1st. Convert the volume and molarity of HCl to the moles of HCl
2nd.Use the balanced equation to convert moles of HCl to the moles of NaOH
3rd. Use the moles and concentration of NaOH to determine the volume of NaOH solution

(grams to volume)
What volume of 0.50 M HCl is required to dissolve 5.6 g of CaCO3
2 HCl(aq) + CaCO3(s) → CaCl2(aq) + H2O(ℓ) + CO2(g)

You are looking for the volume of HCl solution. 

1. converting grams of CaCO3 into moles

2. using the balanced equation to convert moles of CaCO3 to moles of HCl

3. using the concentration of HCl as a conversion factor to convert moles of HCl to volume of HCl solution
