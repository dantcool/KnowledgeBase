Matter can be described by a collection of characteristics called **properties**

**Element** which is the simplest type of matter is a pure substance that cannot be broken down or seperated into simpler substances. 

There is a total of 118 elemts which have been identified, 90 exist in nature and the rest have been synthasized in laboratories. 

**Atoms** is the smallest indivisible unit of an element and individual atoms are too small to be seen with the naked eye or with a standard microscope so we use scanning tunneling microscopy (STM). isolated atoms are spherical and atoms of different elemts vary in different sizes 

Elements are made up of only one type of atom. eg. Oxygen is found in two forms Dioxygen $O_2$ in which two oxygen aoms are grouped together and $O_3$ Ozone in which three oxygen atoms are grouped together. Dioxygen is a ags that makes up about 21% of the air we breathe. Ozone is a gas with a distinct odor that can be toxic to humans. 

**Chemical compound** is a substance formed when two or more elements are combined in a defined ratio. Compounds can be broken down chemically into simpler substances. Table salt is an example of a chemical compound consisting of elements such as sodium, chlorin and phosphoric acid. Soft drinks containe hydrogen, oxygen and phosphorus as well. 

**Molecules** are collectios of atoms that are held together by chemical bonds. The composistion and arrangement of elements in molecules affect the properties of a substtance. For example Water $H_2O$ and hydrogen peroxide $H_2O_2$ contain only the elements hydrogen and oxygen.
