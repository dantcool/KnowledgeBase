i**onic compounds** - contain ions that carry a positive (cation) or a negative (anion), held together by strong attractive forces between cations and anions. 

When a single atom gaions or loses one or more electrons, the number of electrons and protons is no longer equal and a **Monoatomic Ion** is formed.

Elements in the main group of th periodic table (Groups 1A-7A) form monoatmoic ions that have a charge related to the group number of the element 
- Metals in group 1A,2A, and 3A, form cations that have a positive charge equal to the group number of the element 
	- Sodium, Group 1A ($Na^+$ ) Calcium, Group 2A($Ca^{2+}$)
- Nonmetals in groups 5A, 6A, and 7A form anions that have a negative charge equal to the 8 minus the group number of the elemt 
	- Oxygen, Group 6A ($O^{2-}$) Bromine, Group 7A ($Br^-$)
