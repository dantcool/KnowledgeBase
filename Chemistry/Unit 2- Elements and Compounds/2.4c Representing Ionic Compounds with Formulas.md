Ionic compounds are represented by emperical formulas that show the simplest ratio of cations and anions in the compound.  in the formula of an ionic compound the cation symbol or formula is always written first followed by the anion symbol or formula. 

Ionic compounds do not have a positive or negative charge becuae the total cationic positive charge is balanced by the total anionic negative charge makingit possible to predict thge formual of an ionic compound if the cation and anion charges are known. 

- Aluminum is in Group 3A and forms the $Al^{3+}$ ion
- Bromine is in group 7A and forms the $Br^-$ ion
- To form the formula for the ionic compound AlBr3 the +3 cation must be balance by a -3 charge for the overall formula to carry no net charge.  So three Br- ions are needed to balance the 1 Al+3 ion charge.
