
# Components of an Atom 
 Elements are characterized by the number and types of particles of which they are composed of. 
- Atoms - The smalles chemical unit of matter consist of three subatomic particles which are protons neutrons and electrons 
- Protons carry a relative charge of +1 and has a mass of 1.672622 x 10^-24 g.
- Neutrons carry NO electical charge and have a mass of 1.674927 x 10^-24 g
- Electrons carry a relative charge of -1 and have a mass of 9.1099382 x 10^-27 g

# Mass and charge of an Atom 


The mass of an atom is almost entirel accounted for by its dense nucleus of protons and neutrons. The mass of protons and neutrons is so small it is more convenient to define the mass of these particles with the **ATOMIC MASS UNIT(U)** whis is defined as 1/12th the mass of a carbon atom that contains six protons and neutrons. 

Since neutrons and protons ahve similar masses, both have a mass of approximately 1 u. The mass of an electron is about 2000 times less than that of protons and neutrons, and has a mass of approximately zero on the atomic mass unit scale. 

Atoms are neutral because protons and electrons have equal, opposite charges and atoms have equal numbers of positively charged protons and negatively charged electrons. 

An **ION** is a atom with an unequal number of protons and electrons leading to the atom being having an overall positive or negatie charge

When an attom carries more protons leading to an overall positive charge of an atom it is called an **CATION** 

an atom with more electrons than protons leading to an overall negative charge of an atom it is called an **ANION**.
