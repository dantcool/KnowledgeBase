Rules For Naming Ions and Ionic Compounds 
## Monoatomic cations 
- The name of a main-group monoatomic cation is the element name followed by the word ion 
- Na+ sodium ion Mg2+ magnesium ion
- The name of the transition metal catiuon is the element name follwed by the cation charge in roman numerals with parentheses and the word ion (Fe2+ iron(II) ion) (Co3+ covalt(III) ion)

## Monoatomic anions 
- the name of an monoatmoic anion is the element name changed to include the suffixe -ide, follwoed by the word ion
- Br- bromide ion 
- O2- oxide ion
