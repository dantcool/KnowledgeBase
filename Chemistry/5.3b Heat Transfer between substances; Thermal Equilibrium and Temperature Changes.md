When objects at different temperatures come into contact the hotter object transfers thermal energy to the cooler object. This cause the hotter object to cool and the cooler object to warm. This process occurs until the two object reach the same temperature, a state of **Thermal Equilibrium**. Heat lost by the hotter object (-qlost) is equal to that gained by the cooler object(+qgained) 

Example problem 
a 12g block of copper at 12C is immersed in a 5g pool of ethanol with a temperature of 68c. When therm equilibruim is reached what is the temperature of the copper and ethanol.
