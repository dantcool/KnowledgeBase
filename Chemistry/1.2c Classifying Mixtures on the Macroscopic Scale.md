**Mixture** - substance made upof two or more elements or compounds that have not reacted chemically. 

**Homogenous Mixtures** - mixtures that have a constant composition throughtout the material. Example, dissolving tabe salt in water creates a mixture of the two chemical compouns water $H_2O$  and tables salt $NaCl$ because the mixture is uniform, meaning that the same ratio of water to table salt is found no matter where it is sampled it is a Homogenous mixture. 

**Heterogenoues mixture** - mixture in which the composition is not uniform. Example, a cold glass of fresh lemonade with ice is a heterogenous micture because you can see the individual components (ice cubes, lemonade, and pulp) and its relative amount of each component vary on from where the lemonade is sample top of the glass or bottom.
