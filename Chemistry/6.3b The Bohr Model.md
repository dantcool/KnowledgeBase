- according to the model of atomic electronic structure, electrons move around the nucleus of an atom in defined energy levels call orbits.
- Numbers assigned to each energy level are known as **Principle quantum number(n)**
- Electrons in low-energy levels can absorb energy and be promoted to higher-energy levels
- Electrons moving from a high energy level to a lower energy level emit energy int he form of light 
- **ground state** is when an electron is in the lowest energy level, n=1 
- when an atom absorbs energy it moves to an **excited state** where n>1

Bohr's equation which relates the energy (E_2)and energy level(n) for an electron one can calculate the energy of a single electron in a ground or excited state or when an electron moves between two energy levels 

$$E_n=-2.179*10^{-18}J(\frac{1}{n^2})$$
To find the change in ergy from one energy to another we use a variation of Bohr's equation
$$\Delta E=-2.179*10^{-18}J(\frac{1}{n^2_{final}}-\frac{1}{n^2_{initial}})$$
