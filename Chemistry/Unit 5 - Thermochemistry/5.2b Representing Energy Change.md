In these diagrams, the horizontal axis indicates the different states of a system undergoing change or the reactants and products in a reaction. The vertical axis shows the relative enthalpy of each state, which is indicated using a horizontal line. Enthalpy increases as you move up the vertical axis so the higher that line occurs on the y-axis the higher the enthalpy for a given species.
