Standard state conditions for enthalpy values are 
- gases, liquids and solids in their pure form at a pressure of 1 br at a specified temperature 
- solutions with concentration of 1 mol/L at a specified temperature 

$\Delta H^{\circ}_f$ values have units of kJ/mol because each is the enthalpy change for the formation of one mole of a chemical species 

the $\Delta H^{\circ}_f$ value for an element in its standard state is eual to 0kj/mol. Example the reaction fro $\Delta H^{\circ}_f$ of elemental bromis is written
$Br_2(l) \rightarrow Br_2(l)$ there is no change from reactants to products so 
$\Delta H^{\circ}_f$ = 0kj/mol 

most $\Delta H^{\circ}_f$ values are negative. This indicated that for most species the formation from elements in their standard states is an exothermic process
