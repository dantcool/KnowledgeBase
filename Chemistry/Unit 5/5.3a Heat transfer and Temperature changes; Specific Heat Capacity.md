Three factors control the magnitude of a temperature change for an object.
1. the amount of heat energy added to the object 
2. the mass of the object 
3. the material the object is made of 

**Specific Heat Capacity** - the amount of energy needed to raise the temperature of 1 g of a substance by 1 degree Celsius.

$$\text{c, specific heat capacity}(J/g* ^\circ C) = \frac{\text{1, heat energy absorbed (J
)}}{m,mass(g)*\Delta T,change\;in\;temperature(^\circ C)}$$
