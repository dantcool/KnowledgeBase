**Molar Mass** - the mass in grams of one mole of a substance

The molar mass of an element is the mass in grams of one mole of atoms of the element. 
	Example 
$$1C^{12}atom=12u$$
$$6.022*10^{23}(1Mole)C^{12}=12 Grams$$
One mole of an element has a mass in grams equal to its atomic weight in atomic mass units(u). For example the element Magnesium has an atomic weight of 24.31u, one mole of magnesium atoms has a mass of 24.31g.


## Example of Converting between mass and moles of an element

What amount of oxygen, in moles, does 124g O represent

Using the molar mass of Oxygen (1 mol O = 16.00 g) to create a conversion factor that converts mass (in grams) to amount (in mol) of Oxygen.

$$124g\\O\quad\frac{1MolO}{16.00g}=7.75Mol\quad O$$
Is the Answere Reasonable? Since the mass of oxygen in the sample is greater than the molar mass of oxygen, so there is more than 1 mol O in the sample. 

## Example of converting between mass, moles, and atoms of an element.

How many boron atoms are there in a 77.8g sample of elemental boron. 

$$77.8gB*\frac{1molN}{10.81g}*\frac{6.022*10^{23}atomsB}{1molB}=4.33*10^{24}atomsB$$
Is the Answere Reasonable? The mass of the boron sample is greater than the molar mass of boron, So the number of boron atoms in the sample is greater than Avogadro's Number.
