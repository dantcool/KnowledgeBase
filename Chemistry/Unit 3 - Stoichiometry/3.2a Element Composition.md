**Stoichiometry** - The study of the relationship between relative amount of substances. 

The formula of a compound provides information about the relative amount of each element present in either one molecule of the compound or one mole of the compound. 

Example - 1 Molecule of acetic acid, $CH_3CO_2H$ contains two atoms of oxygen and one mole of acetic acid and contains 2 mol of ocygen atoms. 
 
Example with ionic and nonmolecular compounds - Calcium chloride, $CaCl_2$ is not made up of $Cacl_2$ molecules but rather on mole of $CaCl_2$ contains 1 mole $Ca^{2+}$ ions and 2 mol $Cl^-$ ions.
