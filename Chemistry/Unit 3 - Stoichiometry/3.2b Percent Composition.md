Mole-to-Mole relationships in a chemical formula can be used to determine the **Percent composition** of an element in a compound. 

Example of the percent composition of hydrogen in water $H_2O$
$$\frac{2molH(\frac{1.01g}{1molH})}{1molH_2O\frac{18.02g}{1molH_2O}}*100percent = 11.2Percent\;H$$
