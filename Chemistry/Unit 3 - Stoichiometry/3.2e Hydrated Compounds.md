A **hydrated ionic compound** is an ionic compound that has a well defined amount of water trapped within the crystalline solid. The water associated with the compound is called the **water of hydration**. A hydrated compound formula includes the term(nH2O) where n is the number of moles of water incorporated into the solid per mole of ionic compound. Prefixes are used in naming hydrate compounds toi indicate the number of water of hydration.

Heating a hydrated compound releases the water in the crystalline solid. For example heating hte compound $CuCl_2*2H_2O$ Releaseas two mole of water per mole of hydrated compound. 

# Example - Determine the formula of a hydrated compound

a 32.86g sample of hydrate of $CoCl_2$ was heated thoroughly in a porcelain crucible until its weight remained constant. After heating 17.93g of the dehydrated compound remained in the crucible. What is the formula of the Hydrate? 

First determine the mass of water lost when the hydrated compound was heated 

$$32.86g-17.93g=14.93g\;H_2O$$
Next calculate moles of water and moles of the dehydrated compound 
$$14.93h\;H_2O*\frac{1molH_2O}{18.015g}=0.8288mol\;H_2O$$
$$17.93h\;CoCl_2*\frac{1molCoCl_2}{129.84g}=0.1381mol\;CoCl_2$$
Finally dtermine the simplest whole-number ratio of water to dehydrated compound 
$$\frac{0.8288mol\;H_2O}{0.1381mol\;CoCl_2}=\frac{6.001mol\;H_2O}{1mol\;CoCl_2}\approx\frac{6mol\;H_2O}{1mol\;CoCl_2}$$
The chemical formula for the hydrated compound is $CoCl_2*6H_2O$
