- note that the energy of each orbital depends only on its shell not its subshell(true only for hydrogen)
- energy of orbitals for all other atoms depends on both the shell and the subshell 

$$E_n=-2.17*10^{-18}j(\frac{1}{n^2})$$
- when an atom absorbs a photon of light, the electron is promoted to a higher energy level orbital(n>1)
- The photon of light causes the electron to move farther away from the nucleus(Figure Below of a hydrogen atom absorbing energy)
