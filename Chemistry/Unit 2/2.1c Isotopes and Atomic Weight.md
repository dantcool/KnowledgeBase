Even though all atoms of a given element have the same number of protons, the number of neutrons in the nucleus can vary in a particular element. 

Example - although atoms of carbon always have six protons in the nucleus, a carbom atom might have six, seven, or eight neutrons in the nucleus.  

Even though the mass number for the atoms differ becuase of the different number of neutrons, each is still a carbon atom because each has six protons in the nucleus. 

When atoms that have the same atomic number (Z) but have different mass numbers (A) they are called **ISOTOPES**. The convention for naming isotopes is just tagging the mass number with the element name
