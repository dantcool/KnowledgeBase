**Energy** - the ability to do work 
**Work** - The force involved in moving an object some distance
**Thermochemistry** - study of how energy in the for of heat is involved in chemical change 
**Kinetic Energy** - energy associated with motion
**Potential energy** - energy associated with position
**Mechanical energy** - the sum of the kinetic and potential energy of an object as a whole
**Thermal Energy** - the sum of the kinetic and potential energies of all the atoms,molecules, or ions within a system.
**Chemical Energy**- a form of potential energy that can be released when new chemical bonds are formed. 

$$\text{KE = Kinetic Energy}\\KE=\frac{1}{2}mv^2\\Where\\m=mass\\v=velocity$$
potential energy calculations depend on the forces that exist between particles. Because different types of particles experience different types of forces, it is not possible to use a single equation to calculate potential energy.
