Fundamental Duties to Society and public infrastructure 
Guard the public interest and protect the environment, safety, health and welfare of those affected by engineering activities and technological artifacts. Created in 2023?

Electrical -> IEEE
Commit ourselves and agree
- to hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public to strive to comply with ethical design. and sustainable development practices to protect the privacy of others, and to disclose promptly factors that might endanger the public or the environment. (rev 2020)

Chemical Engr -> AICHE 
members shall 
- hold paramount the safety health and welfare of the public and protect the environment in the performance of their professional duties. {shall conveys more commitment than NSPE's "are encouraged to" and its location in the 1st item suggests higher importance }

Mechanical ENGR -> ASME 
- Engineers shall consider environmental impact and sustainable developments in the performance of their professional duties. 

<u>Summary of Coverage of environmental/sustainability in the Code of Ethics</u>
Varying level of inclusion/coverage 
one did mention it (IISE)
Some mentioned it briefly 
(ASME,AICHE,IEEE,INCOSE)
some covered it more extensively (ACM and especially ASCE)\

Also there were varying levels of emphasis 
are encouraged to -> optional/ aspirational?(NSPE)
should -> Recommendation/ideal 
shall -> requirement 
Do -> part of the culture/ expectation (ASCE)

There is much to be learned from ASCE's Code of Ethics, which serves as a model for other disciplines
